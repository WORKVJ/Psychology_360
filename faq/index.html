Help Desk

Got Questions?

Our patient care coordination team is available to assist you with scheduling, billing, or general queries.

What happens in the initial consultation session?

+

The first session centers on intake mapping. We discuss current distress points, personal context background, lifestyle configurations, and establish clinical goals. This aligns client and therapist before starting formal recovery tracks.

How long does a standard therapy session run?

+

A standard individual therapy session runs for 50 minutes. Family systems and couples alignment sessions can range between 75 to 90 minutes depending on intake goals.

Is my personal information fully secure?

+

Yes. Absolute confidentiality is a mandatory legal and ethical requirement in clinical practice. Client consultation records and identity profiles are never shared without explicit, signed consent, unless critical safety risks require intervention.

How can I schedule an appointment with a specific therapist?

+

You can request a specific therapist through our contact page form or phone path. Our coordinator matches client needs and schedules alignments based on specialist calendars.

What is the cancelation policy for scheduled sessions?

+

To avoid scheduling conflicts, we require cancellation alerts at least 24 hours before your session. Cancelations within 24 hours can trigger booking charges.
